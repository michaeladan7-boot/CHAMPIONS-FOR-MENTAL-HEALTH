CHAMPIONS FOR MENTAL HEALTH — MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE

Open index.html to view the homepage.

Pages:
- index.html — Home, preserving the original homepage content areas
- about.html — About, mission, cherished team, gallery and empowerment
- programmes.html — Programmes and services
- research.html — Research and publications
- team.html — Leadership team
- news.html — News and resources
- donate.html — Donations
- volunteer.html — Volunteer form
- contact.html — Contact and enquiry form
- login.html / register.html — Member account screens
- styles.css — Shared design and responsive styles
- script.js — Navigation and demo form feedback

Design direction:
- Uses the reference image as visual inspiration: clean white space, editorial serif headings,
  restrained green accents, soft neutral panels, simple cards and photo-led sections.
- Keeps the client's existing content areas and page structure; adds supporting sections
  rather than removing the original ideas.
- Uses DM Sans for body text and DM Serif Display for large headings.

Before launch:
1. Replace image placeholders with approved organisation photographs.
2. Replace placeholder team names and contact details with client-approved content.
3. Connect contact and volunteer forms to a secure form handler.
4. Connect donations to an approved payment provider.
5. Implement secure authentication before enabling member accounts.
6. Confirm privacy policy, data handling, domain, hosting, backups and SSL.
