Who we are

About Champions for
Mental Health

We use lived experience, advocacy, education and research to help create a world where mental health is understood and supported.

Our story

We believe every person deserves to be heard.

We offer lived experience expertise for co-production, initiate programmes to reduce mental health stigma, teach young people to identify early signs of mental health crises and encourage people to seek help.

We also engage in mental health research and peer research, contributing lived experience perspectives to wider research conversations.

A diagnosis is not a death sentence. There is hope, help and a way forward.
Our mission

Changing the way people see mental health.

We want to reduce stigma, increase understanding and make it easier for people to seek the support they need.
