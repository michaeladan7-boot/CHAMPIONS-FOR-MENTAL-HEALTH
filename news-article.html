Understanding when someone may need support

Noticing when a person needs support, and knowing how to respond with care, can make it easier for them to reach out for help.

Published: add date Author: add author name Reading time: add minutes

Mental health is part of everyone’s life. Learning to notice when a person may need support, and knowing how to respond with care, can make it easier for someone to reach out for help.

Start with a conversation

A calm, respectful conversation is often the first step. Listening without judgement shows that the person is taken seriously and that they are not alone.

A diagnosis is not a death sentence. Help, hope and recovery are possible.

Encourage people to seek help

Encourage the person to talk to someone they trust or to a qualified professional. Small, supportive actions can make a meaningful difference.

  • Listen without judging.
  • Ask how you can help.
  • Encourage them to reach out to a trusted person or professional.

Open conversations help challenge stigma and build communities where people feel safe to ask for support.
